# W47 / Sixth Avenue reference reconstruction

The two added reference screenshots (9:44:05 and 9:44:15 PM) are Google Street View views dated August 2024 at the 1201 Avenue of the Americas / W47 corner. They show the white-ribbed 1185 tower to the south and the 1211 tower/plaza with a blue-lit low pavilion to the north.

- Preserve the official NYC3D source footprints, heights and setbacks.
- `prepare-sixth47.mjs` fits the six visible source wall planes and extracts 119 clipped source wall triangles, including the towers' full heights.
- White tower: narrow glass subdivisions, broad pale pilasters, tall lobby glazing, entrance canopy with bronze trim and warm lighting, revolving-door frames, US/state flags.
- North tower/plaza: warm vertical ribs, dark glass, blue-lit lower pavilion bays, reflective upper pavilion windows, flagpoles, planted bed, four tree planters and benches.
- Streets: three Sabrett-style umbrella carts with dense food photography/menu boards, signal mast arms, pedestrian boxes, one-way/street signs, bins, and the existing equidistant parked vehicles with a dark queue palette.

The earlier W46 frontage remains. Overlapping generic frontage details and screens are replaced only within the W47 corner regions. The photographs supply the target appearance; later proposed renovations on the official property pages are not used to replace this dated reference.
