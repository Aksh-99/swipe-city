# Pub facade texture

Built-in image generation using the supplied pub screenshot as reference. Applied behind the real projecting signs, awnings, flags, wood frames and balcony geometry.

Saved asset: public/storefronts/east45-pub-facades.png

Prompt:

Create a photorealistic architectural facade texture atlas for a 3D game, closely matching the two pubs in this reference. One square image divided into exactly TWO equal width vertical panels: LEFT half is Connolly’s Pub front, RIGHT half is The Perfect Pint front. Both perfectly straight-on orthographic elevations, no perspective. Connolly’s: three storeys, dark reddish-brown elaborately panelled timber facade, four grouped tall narrow sash-window bays on the two upper storeys, deeply carved cornice, warm bar interiors behind dark glass, dark green curved canvas entrance canopy on the ground floor, central sign CONNOLLY’S and PUB & RESTAURANT. Perfect Pint: two-storey dark forest-green historic pub frontage with wide glass upstairs windows, gold serif THE PERFECT PINT lettering, pale carved stone horizontal band above ground-floor entrance, filigree iron balcony at top, warm bar windows. Reference image determines the facade ornament, colours and proportions. Full front elevations edge-to-edge within each half with no space between the halves. Exclude all surrounding taller buildings, sky, rooftop signs, projecting vertical signs, flags, trees, people, cars, street, Google UI, icons, watermarks. Those separate projecting items will be real 3D geometry. Rich photographic wood-grain, aged paint, carved mouldings and reflective window detail. This is a flat material texture, not a scene render or mockup.
