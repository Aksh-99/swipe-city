# Evenly spaced district parking

All W42–49 cross streets and Sixth/Eighth Avenue receive scenic parking on a shared 18-metre longitudinal grid. Slots are omitted beside Times Square, Broadway and Seventh Avenue, within junction approaches and crossings, outside the playable boundary, on sidewalks, or where source geometry, buses, police vehicles, or the W46 construction details occupy the curb. Skipped slots remain gaps; surrounding cars are not shifted off-grid. Existing isolated W46 and Sixth Avenue parking renderers are replaced, avoiding duplicate rows. The older source data remains for regression coverage.

The generated 269 cars use sedan, SUV and van bodies with varied paint. Geometry is batched, and reflected lighting is shared without adding one shader light per car. Parked cars have collision and no transport interaction. The road centre remains clear.
